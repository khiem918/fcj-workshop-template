# Summary Report: "GameDay 8 Đội Tranh Hùng"

### Event Objectives

- Reinforce AWS knowledge through a competitive, game-show style format
- Test participants' understanding across a broad range of AWS services and concepts
- Encourage teamwork and quick decision-making under time pressure
- Create a fun, engaging way to learn AWS instead of a traditional lecture format

### Event Format

- 8 teams competed head-to-head across multiple rounds of AWS-related questions
- Questions covered a broad range of AWS topics: compute, storage, networking, security, databases, and best practices
- Teams earned points for correct and fast answers, with a live leaderboard tracking standings throughout the event
- The competition progressed round by round, narrowing down to the top-performing team by the end

### Key Highlights

#### Broad AWS Knowledge Coverage

- Questions spanned many AWS service categories, testing both breadth and depth of knowledge
- Some questions required quick recall of exact service names, features, and limits
- Other questions tested applied understanding — picking the right AWS service for a given scenario

#### Team Competition Dynamics

- Watching teams strategize and discuss answers under time pressure highlighted different problem-solving approaches
- Close scoring between teams kept the competition exciting all the way to the final rounds
- The head-to-head format made the event much more energetic and engaging than a typical presentation

### Key Takeaways

- Reinforced my own AWS knowledge simply by following along with the questions and answers
- Identified specific AWS topics where my knowledge is weaker and needs further study
- Saw firsthand how a gamified, competitive format makes technical learning more memorable than passive listening

### Applying to Work

- **Build a review checklist**: Use the questions I couldn't answer confidently as a list of AWS topics to study further
- **Practice scenario-based thinking**: Apply the "pick the right service for this scenario" mindset from the game to real project decisions
- **Suggest similar formats internally**: Propose a quiz/game-based format for team knowledge-sharing sessions to make learning more engaging

### Event Experience

Attending **"GameDay 8 Đội Tranh Hùng"** was a fun and high-energy experience that reinforced AWS knowledge in a very different way from a typical workshop. Key experiences included:

#### Learning through competition

- Following along with each question was a great way to self-check my own AWS knowledge in real time.
- Seeing how competing teams reasoned through tricky questions gave me new ways to think about familiar AWS services.

#### Cheering and team spirit

- Supporting a team throughout the rounds made the event feel more like a shared experience than a solo learning session.
- The competitive atmosphere and live leaderboard kept the energy high from start to finish.

#### Networking

- The event was a good opportunity to interact with other participants and teams outside of my usual work circle.
- Discussing answers and strategies with others afterward helped reinforce what I had just learned.
