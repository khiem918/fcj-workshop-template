# Summary Report: "AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application featuring Cloud Kinetics & Renova Cloud"

### Event Objectives

- Provide an in-depth look at career orientation, essential skills, and Cloud Architecture within Vietnam's IT industry
- Give students and beginners a realistic view of the Cloud market, career opportunities, and the skills needed to succeed
- Bridge the gap between foundational knowledge learned at school and hands-on experience in the industry

### Partners

- The event featured shared insights from representatives of **Cloud Kinetics** and **Renova Cloud**
- Included a real-world experience sharing session from a practicing Data Engineer

### Key Highlights

#### Overview of the Cloud market in Vietnam

- **Cloud First trend**: Large enterprises are increasingly prioritizing cloud services, gradually replacing traditional on-premises hardware
- **Opportunities and challenges**: The market has high expectations, but income opportunities are very attractive for those who can prove their capability
- **The role of AI**: AI is changing how we learn and work, boosting productivity; it should be used as a tool to solve harder problems rather than simple exercises
- **A career motto shared at the event**: "Treat your boss and clients like your loved one"

#### Real-world experience from a Data Engineer

- **The gap between school and industry**: School mainly teaches fundamentals, while the industry demands problem-solving thinking and real accountability for actual products
- **Key foundational subjects**: Databases, programming languages, data structures and algorithms, distributed databases, and APIs
- **Systems thinking**: Understanding not just individual components, but how to connect and coordinate services together

#### Overcoming fear and personal growth

- **Facing fear**: Confronting the fear of making mistakes and being judged while presenting
- **Soft skills**: The importance of communication, proactively greeting people, and building relationships with managers and teachers
- **Long-term mindset**: Career orientation isn't about finding a job — it's about finding yourself

### Key Takeaways

#### On the market and technology

- Gained a clearer understanding of the Cloud First trend and why enterprises are shifting strongly toward the cloud
- Recognized AI as a supporting tool rather than a replacement for problem-solving thinking
- Understood the importance of systems thinking when working with Cloud architecture — not just knowing individual services, but how to coordinate them

#### On personal growth and career

- Realized the gap between foundational school knowledge and real industry requirements, and what still needs to be developed
- Learned to view the fear of presenting and communicating as a normal part of personal growth
- Shifted my perspective on career orientation: it's not just about finding a job, but a process of discovering and developing myself

### Applying to Work

- **Apply systems thinking**: When designing or studying Cloud architecture, always ask how services connect and work together
- **Use AI selectively**: Prioritize using AI to help solve complex problems instead of overusing it for simple tasks
- **Practice soft skills**: Proactively communicate, greet people, and build good relationships with colleagues and supervisors during the internship
- **Review foundational knowledge**: Reinforce core subjects (databases, data structures and algorithms, APIs) to better meet real work requirements
- **Build a long-term career mindset**: Treat the internship as part of a journey of self-discovery, not just a line item for a resume
